Description

The tradition Pakistani (specifically from Karachi) style beef biryani is a highly respected standard for biryani. With the perfect mix of beef, white rice, and masala, it's spicy sensations will get you addicted!

So, if you are ready to make your mouth water just at the thought of eating this delicious dish, just follow the recipe below, and you can expect your taste buds tingle by the time you start smelling your wonderful dish.

Ingredients

  • 1.25 lbs beef
  • 1 cup neutral flavored oil
  • 2 large onions sliced thin
  • 2 tbsp, ginger garlic paste
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp coriander powder
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 4 green cardamoms
  • 4 cloves
  • 1/2 cup cilantro
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 cups of long-grain basmati rice (soaked for 30 minutes)
  • pinch of yellow food coloring
  • 1 tbsp rose water

Steps

  1. Start pressure cooker
  2. Fry sliced onions until brown
  3. Add the yogurt, tomato, spices, and masalah
  4. Cook on high heat while stirring for 6 minutes
  5. Now add water (until beef is submerged) and let it cook for 10-15 minutes
  6. Add the potato
  7. Set the masalah aside and start working on the rice
  8. Ready the rice, drain the water
  9. Pour half the rice, then the beef curry
  10. Pour the rest of the rice into a bowl and add the spices, cloves, cardamoms, and coriander powder
  11. Add the food coloring and rose water
  12. Mix, heat as needed, and enjoy!
