Description

Whether you're zoned in at work, relaxing on your armchair at home, or on a calm drive in the night...

...drinking a cup of coffee makes you feel like a king! Here, I share a very standard homemade cold coffee recipe (please try and be impressed...)

Ingredients

  • Grounded Nescafe coffee powder (the one that's been sitting in your cabinet since 1981)
  • Whole milk (the more curdled, the better)
  • Sugar
  • Extra: Flavoring

Steps

  1. Add a tbsp of coffee to a cup
  2. Add as much sugar as you want
  3. Pour in just enough hot water to drown the sugar and coffee
  4. Beat the living unliving out of the sugar and coffee until it mixes
  5. Add in whole milk until the color of the coffee resembles premium wet mud
  6. If you did the extra step, add in whatever flavor you like (I like coffee flavor)
  7. Mix and enjoy!
